Calculation Details, Assumptions & Endurance Models

Step 1 — Equivalent Effort Distance ($D_{\text{eq}}$):

$$D_{\text{eq}} = D_{\text{real}} + \frac{D^+}{100}$$

Example: $30\,\text{km}$ with $2000\,\text{m D}^+$ yields $D_{\text{eq}} = 30 + 20 = 50\,\text{effort-km}$.

Step 2 — Global Intensity Modeling ($\% \text{VMA}_{\text{target}}$):

A. Riegel Model (Exponential Power):

$$\% \text{VMA}_{\text{target}} = \left(\frac{D_{\text{ref}}}{D_{\text{eq}}}\right)^E$$

where $D_{\text{ref}} = 10\,\text{km}$ (reference race where VMA is sustainable at 100%), and $E \approx -0.07$ (fatigue exponent).

B. MIT / CNRS Model (Logarithmic Degradation - Péronnet & Thibault):

$$\% \text{VMA}_{\text{target}} = 100 + IE \cdot \ln\left(\frac{t_{\text{estimated}}}{t_{\text{VMA}}}\right)$$

where $IE$ is the Endurance Index (e.g., $-5.5$), $t_{\text{VMA}} \approx 6\,\text{min}$, and $t_{\text{estimated}}$ is the estimated race time.

C. Daniels / VDOT Model:

$$\% \text{VMA}_{\text{target}} = 0.8 + 0.189439 \cdot e^{-0.012778 \cdot t} + 0.298955 \cdot e^{-0.193260 \cdot t}$$

D. Critical Power (CP - Stryd):

$$P_{\text{target}} = CP + \frac{W'}{t_{\text{estimated}}}$$

E. Fixed Coefficients (Ultra Format > 60km):

Assumes stabilized %VMA around $60\text{--}65\%$ for very long duration efforts.

Step 3 — Segment Speed ($v_{\text{segment}}$) and GAP:

$$\text{Speed}_{\text{uphill}} = \frac{\text{VMA} \times \% \text{VMA}_{\text{target}}}{1 + (\text{Slope \%} \times 0.05)}$$

$$\text{Speed}_{\text{walk D+}} = \frac{\text{VAM (m/h)}}{\text{Slope \%} \times 10} \quad \text{(if Slope} \ge \text{Walk Threshold)}$$

Step 4 — Target HR Calculation (Karvonen):

$$\% \text{Heart Rate Intensity} = \min\left(0.95, \, \frac{\text{Speed}_{\text{segment}}}{\text{VMA}} \cdot \left(1 + |\text{Slope \%}| \cdot 0.02\right)\right)$$

$$\text{HR}_{\text{target}} = \text{HR}_{\text{rest}} + (\text{HR}_{\text{max}} - \text{HR}_{\text{rest}}) \times \% \text{Heart Rate Intensity}$$

Step 5 — Mechanical Power Estimation (Watts):

$$P = M \cdot v \cdot \left(1 + 0.35 \cdot \frac{\text{Slope \%}}{100}\right)$$

where $M$ is total runner mass with equipment (in $\text{kg}$) and $v$ is movement speed (in $\text{m/s}$).

Bibliographic References & Modeling

  • Riegel, P. S. (1981) "Athletic records and endurance." American Scientist, 69(3), 285-290.

    Foundational formula for exponential speed degradation relative to race distance.

  • Péronnet, F. & Thibault, G. (1989) "Mathematical analysis of running performance and world records." Journal of Applied Physiology, 67(1), 453-465.

    Basis of logarithmic degradation modeling based on the Endurance Index ($IE$).

  • Daniels, J. (2013) "Daniels' Running Formula." Human Kinetics.

    VDOT modeling and oxygen uptake for competition pacing.

  • Jones, A. M. & Vanhatalo, A. (2017) "The 'Critical Power' concept: applications to sports performance with a focus on intermittent exercise." Sports Medicine.

    Critical Power ($CP$) framework and anaerobic work capacity ($W'$).

  • Minetti, A. E. et al. (2002) "Energy cost of walking and running at extreme uphill and downhill slopes." Journal of Applied Physiology.

    Energy cost equations per meter of elevation change (Grade Adjusted Pace).
